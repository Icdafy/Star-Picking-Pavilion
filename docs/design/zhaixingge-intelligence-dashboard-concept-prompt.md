## Final prompt

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: shippable high-fidelity desktop web dashboard design image, 16:9 widescreen

Primary request: Design a world-class B2B intelligence dashboard for “摘星阁”, a platform that captures signals across the low-altitude economy and commercial space industries, then uses large language models and algorithmic scoring to surface actionable opportunities and risks. This must look like a real production product, not concept art.

Canvas and viewpoint: one complete straight-on desktop app screen, full-bleed, no device frame, no perspective tilt, no browser chrome. Crisp at first glance and detailed when zoomed.

Information architecture:

- slim left navigation with the product name “摘星阁” and these items: “全域态势”, “情报雷达”, “低空经济”, “商业航天”, “产业图谱”, “AI研判”, “监测任务”
- restrained top bar with global search, industry toggle “低空经济 / 商业航天 / 融合观察”, date and live monitoring status
- page title “全域态势”
- top KPI strip showing “新增信号 12,846”, “高价值情报 382”, “机会线索 64”, “风险预警 27”, “可信信源 92%”
- dominant center-left module “全球信源态势”: elegant dark geospatial world map focused on East Asia, with launch sites, low-altitude demonstration zones, industrial clusters, satellite ground stations, subtle arcs and restrained luminous nodes; clearly distinguish the two industries
- immediately visible AI filtering pipeline: “12,846 原始信号 → 2,107 语义相关 → 382 高价值 → 27 触发预警”, with confidence and evidence-source indicators
- right rail “AI情报流”: 4 compact live event cards with source badges, timestamps, concise Chinese headlines, sector tags, opportunity/risk score, and source confidence; show evidence traceability rather than social-feed styling
- lower modules: “机会雷达” quadrant/scatter chart, “行业热度” dual trend lines, “今日研判” concise model synthesis card with cited source count, and a compact “评分模型” card showing “时效 34% / 可信度 27% / 关联度 21% / 影响力 18%”
- one selected insight card should show “机会评分 87”, “风险评分 42”, “模型置信度 93%”, “18个交叉信源”

Visual direction: premium institutional intelligence terminal; calm, precise, credible, slightly futuristic but fully practical. Sophisticated 12-column grid, exceptional spacing, strong hierarchy, thin separators, softly rounded 10–12px panels, disciplined data density. Dark obsidian and deep navy base, cool cyan and cobalt for low-altitude economy, restrained violet for commercial space, amber for opportunity, coral red only for risk. Subtle fine-grid map texture and tiny star-like data particles, but no decorative space illustration. Mix broad dark surfaces with a few slightly lighter elevated panels; minimal glass effect, no excessive glow.

Typography: modern neutral Chinese sans-serif, highly legible, compact tabular numerals, clear hierarchy. Render the quoted Chinese text verbatim where it appears. Keep all other microcopy minimal and meaningful; no lorem ipsum, no random letters.

Charts and details: accurate-looking axes and legends, tasteful mini sparklines, confidence rings, source chips, tiny provenance icons, event status dots, hover/selection styling implied. Make the AI reasoning and signal-to-decision journey visually obvious.

Constraints: original design only; no third-party logos or trademarks; no people; no photographic hero image; no giant 3D globe; no spaceship illustration; no neon cyberpunk; no excessive glassmorphism; no clutter; no illegible tiny text; no placeholder gibberish; no watermark.
